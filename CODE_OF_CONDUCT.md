# Code of Conduct

agentlab follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be kind, assume good intent, give and take criticism about the work and not the person, and make it easy
for anyone to take part regardless of who they are or how much experience they have. Harassment, personal attacks and
publishing others' private information aren't tolerated.

Report problems privately to the maintainer through GitHub (the profile linked in the repository) or the **Security**
tab's private reporting if it's sensitive. Reports are handled confidentially, and maintainers may remove comments,
commits or contributions that break these rules, or ban people who repeat them.
